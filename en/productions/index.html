Plays

Four original plays rooted in Chhattisgarhi folk theatre. All are available to book for festivals, colleges and venues.

Folk Musical Drama · 2024

Kahani Vasu Ki

A poignant journey of resilience and folk identity. An evocative narrative celebrating the rural landscapes and sal forest valleys of Chhattisgarh, following young Vasu as he navigates the changing tides of tradition and modernity through music, Mandar drums, and living folklore.

  • Direction: Suresh Thakur
  • Duration: 95 mins · Chhattisgarhi / Hindi
  • Audience: All Ages
  • Cast: Anand Verma (Vasu), Pooja Sahu (Aama), Rameshwar Nishad, Devendra Yadav
Recent shows
  • Feb 2025 · Bharat Rang Mahotsav Satellite, Bilaspur
  • Dec 2024 · National Tribal Theatre Festival, Ranchi
  • Oct 2024 · Muktakash Manch, Raipur

Visual Scenography & Drama · 2023

Vincent: A Flashback

A theatrical meditation on passion, madness, and brushstrokes. Blending visual performance art with dramatic monologue, this play explores Van Gogh's tumultuous quest for truth, framed in dialog with Chhattisgarhi indigenous mural sensibilities and earth pigments.

  • Direction: Meenakshi Kashyap
  • Duration: 80 mins · Hindi
  • Audience: 12+
  • Cast: Rajat Sharma (Vincent), Vandana Baghel, Kailash Mandawi
Recent shows
  • Jan 2024 · Kala Academy, Bhopal
  • Nov 2023 · Ambedkar Sanskritic Bhavan, Jashpur

Nacha Folk Satire · 2025

Gabar Ghichor

The timeless folk-farce on migration, motherhood, and justice. Adapted with vivacious Nacha folk comedy, Panthi rhythms, and intense song duels, Gabar Ghichor sheds uncompromising light on migrant labour, rural hypocrisy, and maternal dignity.

  • Direction: Hemant Banjare
  • Duration: 105 mins · Chhattisgarhi
  • Audience: All Ages
  • Cast: Chitra Sinha (Mother), Bhupesh Netam, Sarita Dewangan, Puran Patel
Recent shows
  • Aug 2025 · Rabindra Bhavan, Durg
  • May 2025 · Jashrang National Theatre Festival 2025

Historical Biopic Stage · 2025

Raja Ravi Verma: The Untold Story

Sacred realism, censorship, and the democratization of art. The historic legal and artistic battles of Raja Ravi Varma, exploring the audacity of printing deities for common households while wrestling with royal courts, conservative backlashes, and freedom of expression.

  • Direction: Suresh Thakur
  • Duration: 110 mins · Hindi
  • Audience: 10+
  • Cast: Vikramaditya Rao (Raja Ravi Varma), Snehal Tirkey (Sugandha), Omkar Dhruw
Recent shows
  • Dec 2025 · Jashpur Town Hall Gala Premiere
