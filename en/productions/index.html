🎭 Folk Musical Drama 2024

Kahani Vasu Ki

A poignant journey of resilience and folk identity

An evocative narrative celebrating the rural landscapes and sal forest valleys of Chhattisgarh, following young Vasu as he navigates the changing tides of tradition and modernity through music, Mandar drums, and living folklore.

🎬 Direction: Suresh Thakur
⏱️ Specs: 95 mins • Chhattisgarhi / Hindi
👥 Lead Cast: Anand Verma (Vasu), Pooja Sahu (Aama), Rameshwar Nishad

🎭 Visual Scenography & Drama 2023

Vincent: A Flashback

A theatrical meditation on passion, madness, and brushstrokes

Blending visual performance art with dramatic monologue, this play explores Van Gogh's tumultuous quest for truth, framed in dialog with Chhattisgarhi indigenous mural sensibilities and earth pigments.

🎬 Direction: Meenakshi Kashyap
⏱️ Specs: 80 mins • Hindi
👥 Lead Cast: Rajat Sharma (Vincent), Vandana Baghel, Kailash Mandawi

🎭 Nacha Folk Satire 2025

Gabar Ghichor

The timeless folk-farce on migration, motherhood, and justice

Adapted with vivacious Nacha folk comedy, Panthi rhythms, and intense song duels, Gabar Ghichor sheds uncompromising light on migrant labour, rural hypocrisy, and maternal dignity.

🎬 Direction: Hemant Banjare
⏱️ Specs: 105 mins • Chhattisgarhi
👥 Lead Cast: Chitra Sinha (Mother), Bhupesh Netam, Sarita Dewangan

🎭 Historical Biopic Stage 2025

Raja Ravi Verma: The Untold Story

Sacred realism, censorship, and the democratization of art

The historic legal and artistic battles of Raja Ravi Varma, exploring the audacity of printing deities for common households while wrestling with royal courts, conservative backlashes, and freedom of expression.

🎬 Direction: Suresh Thakur
⏱️ Specs: 110 mins • Hindi
👥 Lead Cast: Vikramaditya Rao (Raja Ravi Varma), Snehal Tirkey (Sugandha), Omkar Dhruw
