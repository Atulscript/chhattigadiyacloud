Editorial · 12 August 2026

Why We Do Theatre in Jashpur: Connecting Land and Stage

By Suresh Thakur

Beyond big metropolitan auditoriums lies the true heartbeat of storytelling. A reflection on five years of independent stage craft amidst the red earth.

Read the full post

When we chose Jashpur as our permanent theatrical home, many questioned why we left the convenience of metropolitan auditoriums. The answer lies in the acoustics of the sal forests, the fearless community feedback at village squares, and the urgency of documenting living folk memory before it gets homogenized.

Workshops · 15 June 2026

Laughter, Clay, and Play: 120 Children at Ullas 2026

By Pooja Sahu

Over three intense weeks, children from diverse socio-economic backgrounds discovered their voice through folk puppetry and theatre exercises.

Read the full post

The highlight of Ullas 2026 was watching children build their own shadow puppets from recycled cardboard and craft Chhattisgarhi folk fables. On final showcase evening, more than 300 parents and village elders filled the open auditorium.
