About us

A theatre and culture group from Jashpur, Chhattisgarh, making plays, festivals, camps and a magazine since 2014.

Our mission

Chhattisgadhiya Cloud is a dedicated Chhattisgarhi art and culture organisation producing theatre, festivals, workshops and publications. We bridge living folk performance traditions with contemporary stagecraft, providing an uncompromised platform for indigenous voices.

Our vision

To establish an autonomous, world-class cultural sanctuary where Chhattisgarhi artists, thinkers, and playwrights create nationally celebrated work while deepening their connection to regional roots.

What shapes our work

Our core differentiator is an uncompromising rootedness in Chhattisgarhi culture: the sharp satire of Nacha, the acrobatic devotion of Panthi, the circular cosmic movement of Karma, and the earthy scenography of raw clay and bamboo.

  • Maati: the soil

    Our stories, songs and satire come from the folk theatre and oral poetry of Chhattisgarh.

  • Prayog: the stage

    We bring those forms to today’s stages with new writing, light and movement.

  • Shilp: the community

    Camps, festivals and the magazine open theatre to children and new audiences.

Directors

  • Suresh Thakur

    Artistic Director & Playwright

    Over 18 years shaping grassroots theatre, writing original scripts, and directing acclaimed productions nationwide.

  • Meenakshi Kashyap

    Festival Curator & Scenographer

    Curates Jashrang National Theatre Festival and designs immersive stages inspired by indigenous architecture and murals.

  • Hemant Banjare

    Folk Traditions & Music Director

    Master of Nacha rhythms and archivist of Central Indian folk balladeers and oral verse.

Our team

  • Rameshwar Mandavi

    Lead Actor & Physical Theatre

    Veteran performer of Nacha acrobatics and lead protagonist in 'Vincent: A Flashback' and 'Karan'.

  • Phoolbasan Netam

    Folk Vocalist & Nacha Performer

    Celebrated voice of Central Indian oral balladeers, blending traditional Suwa and Dadariya with contemporary theatre narratives.

  • Devendra Sai

  • Tushar Dewangan

  • Anupama Beck

  • Punit Ram Yadav

    Mandar & Folk Percussionist

    Over 20 years mastering Mandar, Dholak, and Timki rhythms driving the kinetic velocity of Panthi and Karma theatre.

  • Kiran Toppo

    Community Residency & Youth Coordinator

    Coordinates Ullas Summer Theatre Camp, mobilizing 360+ rural children annually for creative arts immersion.

  • Dr. Prabhat Mishra

    Archive Fellow & Research Editor

    Lead editor of Rang Sandesh monthly review; authors critical treatises on indigenous scenography and folk theatre.

Our journey

  1. 2014

    Founded in Jashpur

    A group of actors and writers set up a non-profit trust to record Chhattisgarhi songs and train young people.

  2. 2018

    Jashrang festival begins

    The first national theatre festival in Jashpur brings visiting groups to local audiences.

  3. 2022

    The magazine launches

    A monthly magazine on theatre, folk arts and literature. Fourteen issues so far.

  4. 2026

    Bilingual and on tour

    Plays tour beyond Chhattisgarh, and our work is now online in Hindi and English.
