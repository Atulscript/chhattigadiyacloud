✦ Artistic Ethos & Institutional Heritage ✦

About Chhattisgadhiya Cloud

A cultural institution producing theatre, national festivals, youth workshops, and intellectual publications — bringing the living spirit of Chhattisgarhi soil to national and global stages.

🏛️ Regd. Cultural Trust
📍 Jashpur & Raipur (C.G.)
⏳ 10+ Years Artistic Practice
🤝 Dept. of Culture Supported
🎯

Our Mission

Chhattisgadhiya Cloud is a dedicated Chhattisgarhi art and culture organisation producing theatre, festivals, workshops and publications. We bridge living folk performance traditions with contemporary stagecraft, providing an uncompromised platform for indigenous voices.

👁️

Our Vision

To establish an autonomous, world-class cultural sanctuary where Chhattisgarhi artists, thinkers, and playwrights create nationally celebrated work while deepening their connection to regional roots.

✦ The Cultural Triad ✦

The Three Pillars of Our Dramaturgy

Organic soil, contemporary scenography, and youth empowerment form the three living axes of our work.

01

🌱 Maati (The Soil)

Direct organic roots in the oral epics, Nacha folk theatre, and ritual rhythms of rural Chhattisgarh.

02

🎭 Prayog (The Stage)

Adapting traditional forms into avant-garde scenography, physical theatre, and national repertory showcases.

03

🎨 Shilp (Community Craft)

Democratising theatre through free youth residencies, puppet craft, and critical public scholarship.

✦ Rooted in the Living Soil ✦

Connection to Chhattisgarhi Culture

Our core differentiator is an uncompromising rootedness in Chhattisgarhi culture: the sharp satire of Nacha, the acrobatic devotion of Panthi, the circular cosmic movement of Karma, and the earthy scenography of raw clay and bamboo.

🌾 Nacha & Gammat Folk Satire 🥁 Panthi & Karma Kinetic Beats 🪔 Dhokra & Bamboo Scenography 🌿 Tribal Oral Ballads & Lore

✦ Our Journey ✦

Milestones in Cultural Preservation

2014 • Trust Foundation

Founding & First Grassroots Folk Workshop (Jashpur)

Established as a registered non-profit cultural trust to document oral Chhattisgarhi songs and train rural youth.

2018 • Jashrang National Festival

Launch of Jashrang National Theatre Festival

Commenced annual winter festival hosting national repertories, tribal bards, and 12,000+ spectators.

2022 • Research & Archive

Monthly Magazine & Digital Cultural Archive

Published 14 volumes of critical essays on folk scenography, tribal oral poetry, and theatrical reform.

2026 • Global & National Outreach

Digital Repertory & Bilingual Cultural Outreach

Expanded bilingual digital repository, touring productions across metro circuits, and inter-state youth residencies.
