Our roots

Three folk traditions shape everything we make.

  • 🎭

    Folk comedy and satire

    Nacha & Gammat

    Open-air folk theatre that uses sharp humour and song to question power.

  • 🥁

    Dance and rhythm

    Panthi & Karma

    Mandar-driven dances that shape our actors' movement and energy.

  • 🏺

    Craft and design

    Dhokra & Murals

    Bell metal, bamboo and ochre murals inspire our sets and costumes.
