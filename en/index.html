New issue · September 2026

Folk Theatre in Modern Times: Continuity, Dance & Scenography

Nacha on city stages, Habib Tanvir's bamboo scenography and the hill-valley ballads of Jashpur — this month we follow Chhattisgarh's folk forms as they meet contemporary audiences.

Read 5 pages free Buy · ₹99

Our roots

Three folk traditions shape everything we make.

  • Folk comedy and satire

    Nacha & Gammat

    Open-air folk theatre that uses sharp humour and song to question power.

  • Dance and rhythm

    Panthi & Karma

    Mandar-driven dances that shape our actors' movement and energy.

  • Craft and design

    Dhokra & Murals

    Bell metal, bamboo and ochre murals inspire our sets and costumes.
