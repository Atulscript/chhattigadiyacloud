Every year in Jashpur

Festivals

Two free festivals in Jashpur every year: Jashrang for theatre, Kavita Utsav for poetry.

Next edition · 2026

Jashrang National Theatre Festival

A premier annual celebration of Indian theatre in Jashpur, uniting prestigious troupes, folk ensembles, and avant-garde directors from across the country.

  • November 14–18, 2026 · Roots & Horizons (माटी और विस्तार)
  • Jashpur Auditorium & Open-Air Stage, Chhattisgarh
  • Free entry
Programme
  1. Day 1 · 6:00 PMInaugural Ceremony & Karma Folk CircleJashpur Folk Troupe
  2. Day 2 · 7:00 PMGabar GhichorChhattisgadhiya Cloud Ensemble
  3. Day 3 · 6:30 PMAndha Yug (Contemporary Adaptation)Bhopal Repertory
  4. Day 4 · 7:00 PMCharandas Chor RevivalNaya Theatre Legacy Ensembles
  5. Day 5 · 5:30 PMNational Directors Conclave & Valedictory GalaAll Participating Artists
Reserve free pass Apply to perform

Next edition · 2026

Jashpur Kavita Utsav

An annual gathering of poets, bards and oral storytellers in Jashpur, celebrating Chhattisgarhi, Hindi, Kurukh and tribal poetry.

  • October 02–03, 2026 · Songs of Forest & People (जंगल और जन के गीत)
  • Ambedkar Sanskritic Bhavan, Jashpur
  • Free entry
Programme
  1. Day 1 · 11:00 AMTribal Oral Poetry SymposiumElders & Folk Lyricists
  2. Day 1 · 5:00 PMJashpur Sham-e-Shayari & Geeth SandhyaRegional Poets
  3. Day 2 · 10:30 AMYouth Open-Mic & Slam Poetry RoundStudent Delegates
  4. Day 2 · 4:00 PMGrand Chhattisgarhi Kavi SammelanEminent National Guests

Plan your visit

Jashpur is in the hills of north-east Chhattisgarh. Nearest railway stations: Raigarh (160 km) and Ranchi (150 km). Nearest airports: Ranchi and Raipur.
