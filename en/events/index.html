✦ 🎭 Flagship National Theatre Conclave ✦

Jashrang National Theater Festival

Annual Sanctuary of Indian Theatre in Jashpur

A premier annual celebration of Indian theatre in Jashpur, uniting prestigious troupes, folk ensembles, and avant-garde directors from across the country.

🎭 12 National Plays 🎪 5 Festival Days 👥 6,500+ Spectators 🎟️ Free Public Entry

✦ 📜 Oral Bardic & Poetry Conclave ✦

Jaspur Kavita Utsav

Conclave of Poets, Bards & Oral Balladeers

Built with the year-tile drill-down from the start, celebrating Chhattisgarhi, Hindi, Kurukh, and regional tribal poetic traditions in the lap of nature.

📜 40+ Invited Bards 🎶 Flute & Mandar 🌿 Indigenous Dialects 🎤 Youth Open Mic

Visitor Travel & Passes Guide

Plan Your Cultural Journey to Jashpur

Located amidst the scenic hills and waterfalls of north-eastern Chhattisgarh. Nearest major railheads: Raigarh (160 km) & Ranchi (150 km). Nearest airports: Ranchi (IXR) & Raipur (RPR). Public entry to open stages is complimentary.
